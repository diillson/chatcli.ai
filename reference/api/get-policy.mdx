---
title: "Get Policy"
openapi: "GET /policies/{kind}/{name}"
description: "Returns one ApprovalPolicy, NotificationPolicy, EscalationPolicy or IncidentSLA with its raw spec and status"
icon: "file-lines"
---

<ParamField path="kind" type="string" required>
  `approval`, `notification`, `escalation` or `sla`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>
  Policy name
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="namespace" type="string">
  Namespace of the policy. When omitted, every namespace is searched and the first policy with that name is returned.
</ParamField>

Minimum role: `viewer`. Read-only: any method other than `GET` returns `405`. `spec` and `status` are the CRD fields unchanged; `resourceMeta` carries name, namespace, UID, creation time, labels and annotations.
