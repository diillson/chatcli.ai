---
title: "List Runbooks"
openapi: "GET /runbooks"
description: "Returns the list of Runbook resources"
icon: "list"
---

<ParamField query="namespace" type="string">
  Only runbooks in this namespace. Omit to list every namespace.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">
  Page number
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="pageSize" type="integer" default="20">
  Items per page (maximum 100)
</ParamField>

Each item is a `Runbook` custom resource. A runbook is offered for an incident when its `trigger` (`signalType`, `severity`, `resourceKind`) matches the incident; its `steps` become the actions of the RemediationPlan. No other filters are supported.
