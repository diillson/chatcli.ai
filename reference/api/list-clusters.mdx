---
title: "List Clusters"
openapi: "GET /clusters"
description: "Returns the ClusterRegistration resources of a federated environment"
icon: "list"
---

<ParamField query="namespace" type="string">
  Filter by the namespace where the ClusterRegistration lives. Omit it to list every namespace.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">
  Page number
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="pageSize" type="integer" default="20">
  Items per page (maximum 100)
</ParamField>

Minimum role: `viewer`. There are no status or region filters; use [Federation Clusters](/reference/api/federation-clusters) to filter by tier.

| Field | Source |
|-------|--------|
| `displayName`, `region`, `environment`, `tier` | `spec` of the ClusterRegistration (`environment`: `prod`, `staging`, `dev`; `tier`: `critical`, `standard`, `non-critical`) |
| `connected`, `lastHealthCheck`, `nodeCount`, `namespaceCount`, `activeIssues`, `activeRemediations` | `status`, written by the federation controller's health checks |
| `version` | `status.kubernetesVersion` |
| `labels` | `metadata.labels` |

Zero-valued and empty fields are omitted (`connected` is always present).
