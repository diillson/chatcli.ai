---
title: "Get SLO Error Budget"
openapi: "GET /slos/{name}/budget"
description: "Returns the error budget numbers of an SLO"
icon: "coins"
---

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>
  SLO name (e.g., `payment-availability`)
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="namespace" type="string">
  Namespace of the SLO. When omitted, every namespace is searched.
</ParamField>

Minimum role: `viewer`. The response has only `spec` (no `status` or `resourceMeta`) and every field is always present. There is no history, forecast or granularity option.

- `currentValue` and `errorBudgetRemaining` are percentages; `errorBudgetTotal` is a fraction (`1 - target/100`) and `errorBudgetUsed` is the used part in the same unit.
- `burnRate` is the controller's 1-hour burn rate (`status.burnRate1h`; `1.0` = consuming the budget exactly on schedule). The 6h, 24h and 72h rates are in [Get SLO](/reference/api/get-slo).
- `state` is `Healthy`, `AtRisk` or `Breached` (see [List SLOs](/reference/api/list-slos)), and empty before the controller's first calculation.

</ResponseExample>
