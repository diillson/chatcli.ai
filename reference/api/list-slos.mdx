---
title: "List SLOs"
openapi: "GET /slos"
description: "Returns the list of ServiceLevelObjective resources"
icon: "list"
---

<ParamField query="namespace" type="string">
  Filter by Kubernetes namespace. Omit it to list every namespace.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="page" type="integer" default="1">
  Page number
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="pageSize" type="integer" default="20">
  Items per page (maximum 100)
</ParamField>

Minimum role: `viewer`. There are no service or status filters: the list is filtered only by namespace and paginated.

Field notes:

- `sli` is `spec.indicator.type` (`availability`, `latency`, `error_rate` or `throughput`); `target` and `window` come from `spec.target.percentage` and `spec.target.window`.
- `currentValue` and `errorBudgetRemaining` are converted to **percent** (the CRD stores them as fractions); `errorBudgetTotal` is returned as stored, a fraction (`1 - target/100`).
- `errorBudgetUsed` and `state` are read from `status.errorBudgetUsed` and `status.state`, which the SLO controller does not write in the current release, so they are omitted (zero-valued fields are left out of the JSON). Use `kubectl get slo -o yaml` for `errorBudgetConsumedPercentage`, `targetMet` and the `burnRate1h`/`6h`/`24h`/`72h` fields.
