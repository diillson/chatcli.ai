---
title: "Analytics Trends"
openapi: "GET /analytics/trends"
description: "Returns issue counts over time with severity breakdown, useful for spotting patterns"
icon: "arrow-trend-up"
---

<ParamField query="from" type="string">
  Start of the window, RFC 3339 (e.g., `2026-03-01T00:00:00Z`). An unparseable value is ignored.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="to" type="string">
  End of the window, RFC 3339.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="groupBy" type="string" default="day">
  Group results by `day` or `week`. Any other value groups by day.
</ParamField>

Minimum role: `viewer`. Covers every namespace. Issues are bucketed by `creationTimestamp`; buckets are sorted by date and only buckets with at least one Issue are returned. `bySeverity` only lists severities that occurred.

With `groupBy=week`, the bucket `date` is January 1 of the ISO year plus `(ISO week − 1) × 7` days, an approximation that does not always fall on a Monday.
